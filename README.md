### 🧠 ClassPilot AI

A college-focused platform designed to make academic management smarter.

**Working on:**

* 🎓 Role-based college management
* 👨‍🏫 Teacher & student management
* 📊 Attendance management
* 📚 Subjects, semesters & departments
* 📝 Class sessions & notes
* 🤖 AI-assisted note verification
* 🔐 JWT authentication & protected APIs
* 🗄️ MongoDB-based backend

### 🌱 Native Healthy (NATHY)

A startup idea focused on bringing India's traditional health drinks into a modern, sustainable ecosystem.

The idea combines:

* 🌴 Direct sourcing from farmers
* 🧃 Modern product & packaging concepts
* 🚚 Tree-to-bottle cold-chain thinking
* 🌾 Farmer-focused business model
* 💡 Technology + traditional products

> **Goal:** Connect traditional Indian products, farmers and modern consumers through technology and thoughtful product design.
